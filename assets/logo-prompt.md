## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one original minimalist app logo for BrightnessTray, a lightweight Windows display brightness synchronizer. Inspiration: the simplicity, geometric balance and interlocking visual rhythm of the ChatGPT logo, but invent a distinct original mark, do not reproduce its six-loop knot. A compact bold circular aperture made of three interlocking rounded ribbon arcs around one clear central light opening, expressing balanced light shared by multiple displays. Extremely simple silhouette, uniform thick strokes, few large negative spaces, recognizable at 16x16 and 24x24. One flat solid near-black ink color, transparent background, no text, no letters, no gradients, no shadows, no tiny details, no mockup, no border, no extra marks. Single centered mark occupying 85% of a square canvas. Precisely clean antialiased edges.
