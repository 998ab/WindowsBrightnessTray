# BrightnessTray v0.2.4

Compact controls with room for complete text.

- Reduce the two-monitor linked popup from 352 × 384 to 320 × 250 DIPs, and Settings from 352 × 484 to 320 × 348 DIPs. Font sizes are preserved.
- Tighten monitor rows, headers, sliders, and settings groups without removing controls.
- Size calibration descriptions, status messages, and action buttons using the actual localized font metrics. The wizard adapts to its content instead of reserving a fixed 640-DIP height.
- Keep comparison strips clear of the wizard on stacked displays, and scroll focused calibration sliders into view when space is limited.
- Scroll the full calibration page when a small screen at high DPI cannot fit even the minimum layout; keyboard focus reveals action buttons as well as sliders.
- Reserve a separate measured area for errors and keep the entire no-monitor hint visible, including with synchronization enabled.
- Add layout checks for English and Russian, light and dark themes, and 100%, 125%, 150%, and 200% DPI, including every wizard step and a stacked-display arrangement.

The dimensions above use two monitors and the default font settings. Calibration height varies with language and the available work area.
